# WorkflowPilot Lab content rules
Audience: English-speaking freelancers and solo business owners. Tone: clear, specific, neutral, budget-conscious.
Do not fabricate first-hand testing, prices, free-plan limits, screenshots, or affiliate relationships. Verify pricing/availability from official pages before publishing.
Each article: clear search intent, useful original examples, limitations, dates, relevant internal links, and clear affiliate disclosure close to affiliate links.
Do not add analytics or cookies without updating the privacy notice. Keep pages fast, responsive, and accessible.
