## Corrections come first

If a guide here is out of date or wrong, that is the message we most want to receive.
Japanese fares, procedures and tax rules change at short notice, and a visitor acting on
a stale page can lose real money at an airport counter. Tell us what you found and where,
and the page will be corrected with the date noted.

## Email

**afiri1014@gmail.com**

Please include the page title or URL you are writing about.

## What we can and cannot answer

We can help with: a guide that contradicts an official page, a broken or outdated link, a
step that no longer matches what you saw, or a partnership and advertising enquiry.

We cannot help with: your visa or immigration status, your specific tax situation, lost
property, bookings made with another company, or anything requiring a decision about your
individual circumstances. For those, go to the official body linked in the relevant guide.

## Response time

Written by one person in Japan, so replies are not instant. Corrections are prioritised
over everything else.
