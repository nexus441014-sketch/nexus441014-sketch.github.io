Japan Explained is written from Japan, for people visiting it for the first time.

## What this site is for

Most Japan travel content answers "where should I go". This site answers the duller
question that actually derails trips: **how does this thing work, and what do I have to
decide before I arrive?** Connectivity, rail passes, IC cards, payment, luggage, the
tax-free counter. The parts that are obvious to residents and invisible to visitors.

## How these guides are written

- Rules, fares and procedures are checked against the operator's or the government's own
  page, and each guide links that source so you can recheck it yourself.
- Prices and timetables are deliberately left out where they change often. A number that
  is three months stale is worse than no number, because you will plan around it.
- Where something genuinely varies by airport, region, season or nationality, the guide
  says so instead of giving one answer that is wrong for half of readers.
- Every guide carries the date it was last reviewed.

## What this site will not do

- Claim to have tested a service it has not used.
- Reproduce a price or an opening time it cannot source.
- Give visa, immigration, tax or legal advice. Those depend on your nationality and
  circumstances: the official links in each guide are the authority, not this site.
- Recommend something only because it pays a commission.

## Corrections

Japan changes its rules more often than visitors expect, and pages here will sometimes
fall behind. If you find something out of date or wrong, please say so on the
[contact page](/contact.html). Corrections are made with the date noted.
